# A design system of its own

Author's instruction: create a design system like the one in luu (`jgermade/luu`, `web/theme/default`), with a style of its own.

## 2026-11-11: The ledger

What is taken from luu: a theme is a folder with its tokens, its components and a page that shows them. `tokens.css` is the only place each value is written, and components read the tokens with no fallback. A component imports nothing, takes everything as props or slots, gives values back through `:model`/`$updateModel` and `$emit`, and scopes its styles. `prefers-reduced-motion` zeroes the durations once, in the tokens. `data-tone` makes an island of the other tone.

What is its own: the look of a ledger ("libro mayor"), from what vericount is (bookkeeping, chained records, the abacus-and-blocks logo):

- Warm paper grounds, ink in the logo's blue, hairline rules, the red double margin rule of a ledger page (`Card ruled`).
- A book serif (system fonts only: the CSP loads nothing from elsewhere) for titles and the figure a page is about; tabular, lining figures with a slashed zero for every sum; mono for hashes.
- Thumb-sized controls (36/44/52 px): the app is used on a phone.
- Flat sheets: a hairline under them instead of a glow.
- Domain components: `Amount` (cents a step smaller, currency muted, negatives red), `Hash` (groups of four, short or full, copies whole), `Chain` (the records as the logo's blocks on a rail, coloured by AEAT state), `Stamp` (rubber stamp: ANULADA, FACTURA DE PRUEBA, VERIFICADA; prints in colour), `Entry` (a ledger line with tags and a figure aligned on the right), `Tile`.
- General ones: `Button`, `Badge`, `Alert`, `Card`, `Field`, `TextInput`, `Select`, `Checkbox`, `Segmented`, `FileInput` (drop zone, for the certificate), `Confirm` (the in-place question before an irreversible action; the harmless answer takes the focus), `TabBar` (the logo's block marks the current section), `Spinner` (three blocks of the chain).

Layout: `web/src/theme/tokens.css`, `components/*.html`, `Demo.html`, `demo.ts`, `demo.css`; the page is `web/design/index.html`, a second Vite entry, served at `/design/` by the server like the rest of `web/dist`.

Decisions:

- Token prefix `--vc-`. Light on `:root`; dark from `prefers-color-scheme` unless `data-theme="light"`, or with `data-theme="dark"` (the dark block is written twice for that).
- Components write no text: every label comes in props or slots, so the app's texts stay in `es.ts`. The demo page is for whoever builds the app and is in English, like this record; its sample data is Spanish.
- TypeScript: components use `<script :setup="{ … }: Props" lang="ts">` with defaults; checked to build and run precompiled under the server's CSP.
- No `:style` bindings: `style-src 'self'` refuses style attributes. TabBar sizes its columns with `grid-auto-flow`; the demo paints swatches through the CSSOM.
- Found: a component prop called `value` never arrives when bound as `:value` (jq79 sets the DOM property), so `Amount` takes `amount` and `Hash` takes `hash`. Also, an inline handler with two statements (`a++; b = false`) only ran partly; the demo uses a function.

Checked: `npm run build` (type check included); the demo through the real server and its CSP, in light and dark at 1280 px and at 390 px (no horizontal scroll); bindings of every form component, Confirm's focus and events, TabBar, Entry clicks.

Not done, as in luu: the app does not use the theme yet. Moving the screens over (App, Home, Invoices, Contacts, Settings, SignIn) and retiring `app.css` is the next step, one screen at a time.

## 2026-11-11: Away from the ledger, towards a money app (in progress)

Author's feedback: the interface must not look like an invoice; they like Revolut's style. The direction taken (the language of a money app, not Revolut's brand): light grey page with white cards and no shadows, large radii (8/14/22 px), pill and round controls 48 px high, one vivid accent (`#3d5afe`, dark `#6b83ff`), a true black dark mode with cards a step lighter, one system sans in two voices (text, and a heavier, tighter display for headings and the figure a screen is about), big bold amounts with smaller cents.

Done so far, committed and building:

- `tokens.css` rewritten. Renamed: `paper` → `bg`, `ink*` → `fg*`, `rule` → `line`, `font-serif` → `font-display`; removed: `margin`, `font-stamp`, `stamp-opacity`, `tracking-caps`; new: `weight-heavy`, `tracking-display`, `avatar`.
- `Stamp` removed (a rubber stamp is what made it look like an invoice; the printed invoice keeps its own stamp in `app.css`).
- Restyled: `Card` (no `ruled` any more), `Tile`, `Entry` (round avatar with `initials` or the `icon` slot, `tone`), `Alert` (round icon, soft card). New: `RoundAction` (round button with its label under it, for a screen's main actions).
- `Demo.html` updated just enough to build (Stamp and ruled cards gone).

Still to do, in this order:

1. Restyle the rest to the new language: `Button` (pill; secondary as a soft accent tint, danger as a soft red), `TextInput` and `Select` (filled, no border, focus ring), `Segmented` (pills), `TabBar` (no block mark; the active item in the accent), `Confirm`, `FileInput`, `Checkbox`, `Badge`, `Amount` (heavier whole part), `Chain`, `Hash`.
2. Rework `Demo.html`: a home screen with a big balance (the quarter's total) and a row of `RoundAction`s, invoice list as Entries with avatars, and the screens section without the invoice look. Check in light and dark, at 390 px and 1280 px, through the real server (CSP).
3. Then apply the theme to the app's screens, one domain at a time (see [`2026-11-11.code-by-domain`](2026-11-11.code-by-domain.WIP.md)), retiring `app.css` except the printable invoice.
