Colours

By role, never by hue: --vc-bg, not "beige". Every one is redefined for the dark tone, and any element can be an island of the other tone with data-tone.

Type

One family, the system's, in two voices: text, and display for headings and the figure a screen is about, heavier and a little tight. Mono for hashes. No web fonts: the CSP loads nothing from elsewhere.

Figures

Tabular and lining: a column of sums lines up digit by digit; the cents a step smaller.

Shape & motion

Generous round corners, pill-shaped controls 48 px high, thumb-sized. Cards sit on the grey without a shadow; only what floats has one. The rounded square is the logo's, for the chain of records.
